About

Often called the diva of the Pacific, Vanuella Watt brings her powerful, luminous voice to Charles Gounod's "Ave Maria", one of the most beloved sacred pieces ever written.

From the first note to the final, soaring phrase, this live performance is about stillness, devotion and the sheer presence of a voice that fills a room. Headphones on, lights down.

Gounod's melody floats above the first prelude of Johann Sebastian Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier, a pairing he first published in the 1850s and one that has been sung at ceremonies and concerts ever since.
