About the Song

ANGEL is a sacred invocation born from the darkest hours — a prayer for resurrection, a call to rise again. When the weight of the world becomes unbearable, when darkness feels eternal, there is a voice that calls you back to the light.

Written and performed by Vanuella Watt, ANGEL channels the sacred traditions of the Pacific Islands into a spiritual soul anthem that transcends borders and touches the deepest parts of the human spirit.
