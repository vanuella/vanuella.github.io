About

To open Pink October, Vanuella Watt sings "La Vie en Rose", the love song Edith Piaf made famous around the world. She performs it live in a pink dress, with the lights of Paris shining through the window behind her.

Pink October is the month for breast cancer awareness, and for holding the people we love a little closer. Vanuella brings her Pacific soul to Piaf's classic as a reminder to see life in pink: with love, with music, and with hope.

“I want love, pink, love, and music in your life! And your little blue thumb if you like it! Thank you for being there.”

— VW
