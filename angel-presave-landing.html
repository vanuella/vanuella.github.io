About the Artist

Vanuella WATT is a soul and spiritual artist from New Caledonia, channeling the sacred traditions of the Pacific Islands into music that transcends borders and touches the spirit.

Her sound is not designed to trend—it is crafted to heal, to resurrect, to remind us of the light that exists within even the darkest moments.
