🌊 A Journey into the Soul of Another

Moved by her father's irreversible illness, the singer has chosen a perspective of rare empathy. Rather than singing of her own grief, she steps into the shoes of the one fading away. She explores the inner world of a soul imprisoned in a declining body, attempting to translate what words can no longer express.

This track explores the profound solitude inherent in incurable diseases — that moment when the world keeps turning while the patient sinks into an inner silence.

💜 Love Beyond Memory

The song reminds us with heartbreaking sweetness that even as health fails and memory fades, the essence of the being remains.

Health weakens,
but the bond persists.

Memories vanish,
but the emotion stays intact.

It highlights an essential truth: love is the last thing that remains when everything else has been stripped away by illness.

🕯️ A Cry Against Oblivion and Taboo

Beyond the personal tribute, this song carries a crucial social dimension for our region. All too often, in our islands, these incurable illnesses remain shrouded in mystery or excessive privacy. This public silence leaves families and patients in an even more cruel state of isolation.

"This is a song to ensure that
no one is forgotten in the shadows of illness."

🌺 A Call for Solidarity

Through her voice, the Diva issues a vibrant plea:

👁️ Understanding
Looking at illness without turning our eyes away.

🤝 Solidarity
Supporting families going through the journey of "anticipatory grief."

❤️ Unconditional Love
Accompanying those we know we are losing, ensuring their journey is surrounded by human warmth.

This song is an outstretched hand — a reminder that even in the face of the inevitable, we have the power to ensure no one faces the darkness alone. It is a necessary work of art, where sadness gives way to the beauty of resilience.
